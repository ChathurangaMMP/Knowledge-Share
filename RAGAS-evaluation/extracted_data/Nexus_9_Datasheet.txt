1. Architecture Overview

The Nexus-9 QPU introduces the new "Hyper-State" architecture, designed to stabilize

qubit coherence at room temperatures for up to 50 milliseconds. Unlike the previous
Nexus-8 model, which utilized a purely superconducting loop, the Nexus-9 integrates a

hybrid photonic-trap interface. This allows for a 40% reduction in error rates during gate
operations.


The system is comprised of three core modules:

     1.  The Core Lattice:        Houses the 128 physical qubits.

     2.  The Control Plane:         Manages microwave pulse generation.

     3.  The Readout Layer:          Converts quantum states into digital signals via the PCIe 6.0

         interface.

hardware damage.

3. Cooling System Requirements (The "Cryo-Stack")

The cooling system operates in four distinct stages. It is crucial that the stages are

initialized in this specific order to prevent thermal shock to the ceramic interposers.

     •   Stage 1: Pre-Cooling (Ambient to 4K)

              o   Uses a pulse-tube cryocooler.

              o   Duration: 12 hours minimum.


              o   Note: Do not activate the Control Plane during this stage.

     •   Stage 2: Condensation (4K to 1.2K)

              o   Uses the Joule-Thomson expansion valve.

              o   Pressure must be maintained at 14 bar.

     •   Stage 3: Dilution (1.2K to 100mK)


              o   Circulates the Helium-3/Helium-4 isotope mix.

              o   The "Mix Chamber" valve must be opened to 45%.

     •   Stage 4: Hyper-Cooling (100mK to 15mK)

              o   Activates the demagnetization reducers.


              o   CRITICAL:      Once Stage 4 begins, the system cannot be powered down for
                  4 hours.


4. Troubleshooting & Error Codes

The following codes may appear on the Readout Layer during initialization.

     •   ERR-404 (Coherence Loss):              Usually caused by vibration exceeding 0.05 g.
         Check isolation mounts.


     •   ERR-502 (Thermal Runaway):               Indicates a failure in Stage 3 Dilution. Check the
         He-3 isotope levels.

     •   ERR-909 (Voltage Spike):           Detected in the Control Plane. Immediate shutdown

         required.
